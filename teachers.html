Circle Time with Ian: free resources for teachers

Short, calm cartoon lessons for nurseries, preschools, church schools and children's homes. Every episode comes with a free lesson plan and a free printable page.

How to use Ian in class

  1. Ask the "before you press play" question to wake up curiosity.
  2. Play the episode on a classroom screen (5 to 15 minutes). Pause at the questions.
  3. Do the hands-on activity, then hand out the free printable page.

FREE Episode 1

Ian's Zoo Day

Goal: Children name common zoo animals and say one thing about each.

Before you press play

  • Ask: who has been to a zoo? Which animals live there?

Pause and ask

  • Which animal is that?
  • What sound does it make?
  • Is it big or small?

After the episode

  • Ask each child: which animal was your favourite, and why?

Activity

Animal walk. Call out an animal from the episode and let the children move like it: stomp, hop, waddle or swing. No materials needed.

FREE Episode 2

Ian's Little Garden

Goal: Children tell the order: seed, soil, water, sun, plant.

Before you press play

  • Show a real seed. Ask: what do you think will grow from this?

Pause and ask

  • What does a plant need to grow?
  • What did Ian do to help?
  • What grows from a seed?

After the episode

  • Ask the children to tell the steps back in order, using their hands to show the plant growing.

Activity

Cup garden. Each child puts a bean seed on wet cotton in a clear cup by the window. Check it every morning and talk about what changed.

FREE Episode 3

Ian's Shape Hunt

Goal: Children recognise and name a circle, a square and a triangle.

Before you press play

  • Draw a circle, a square and a triangle on the board. Ask: do you know these shapes?

Pause and ask

  • What shape is that?
  • How many corners does a triangle have?
  • Can you see that shape in our room?

After the episode

  • Ask: which shape has no corners? Which one has four?

Activity

Classroom shape hunt. Draw one big shape on each of three sheets of paper. Children find objects in the room and place them on the matching sheet.

FREE Episode 4

Ian's Number Walk

Goal: Children count objects from 1 to 5, one number for each object.

Before you press play

  • Hold up your fingers one at a time and count to five together.

Pause and ask

  • How many can you see?
  • Can you count with Ian on your fingers?
  • What number comes after 3?

After the episode

  • Line up five toys and count them together. Take one away and count again.

Activity

Counting walk. Walk around the playground and collect five leaves, stones or sticks each. Sit in a circle and count them out loud together.

FREE Episode 5

Ian's Color Day

Goal: Children name six colors: red, blue, yellow, orange, green and purple.

Before you press play

  • Hold up a red, a blue and a yellow crayon. Ask: who knows these colors?

Pause and ask

  • What color is that?
  • Can you find something in our room that is the same color?
  • What is your favourite color so far?

After the episode

  • Ask each child to name one color from the episode and point to something that color.

Activity

Color sort. Put a pile of crayons, blocks or paper scraps in the middle. Children sort them into six groups, one for each color, and name each group together.

FREE Episode 6

Ian's Snowy Day

Goal: Children name winter clothes (hat, scarf, gloves, boots, coat) and say why we wear them when it is cold.

Before you press play

  • Show a hat, a scarf or a glove. Ask: when do we wear this? Is it for a hot day or a cold day?

Pause and ask

  • What is falling outside the window?
  • What does Ian put on to stay warm?
  • What did they make with the snow?

After the episode

  • Ask each child to name one thing Ian wore in the snow and point to where it goes on the body.

Activity

Dress for snow. Put a pile of hats, scarves, gloves and coats from the dress-up box or lost-and-found in the middle. Children take turns putting on one item and saying its name, then the class counts how many items the child is wearing.

FREE Episode 7

Ian's Christmas Eve

Goal: Children name Christmas things like a tree, star, bell, lights, present, ribbon and bow, and say what we do with them.

Before you press play

  • Show a small wrapped box or a piece of ribbon. Ask: what is this? When do people give presents?

Pause and ask

  • What is Ian putting on the tree?
  • Can you find a star or a bell?
  • What do we use to wrap a present?

After the episode

  • Ask each child to name one thing from the episode they would put on a Christmas tree.

Activity

Wrap a present. Give each pair of children a small box or block, a sheet of scrap paper and a piece of string. They wrap it together, tie a bow, and say each step out loud: box, paper, string, bow.
