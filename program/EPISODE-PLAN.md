==================================================
1. WHAT THIS IS
==================================================
A long-term program, not a loose list. The channel teaches English to children aged 2 to 6.
- 12 WORLDS a year: one world (one big theme) every calendar month, the same month every year.
- 3 LEVELS: Year 1 = words. Year 2 = short sentences with the same worlds. Year 3 = little stories, questions and answers.
- Every month: 4 weeks = 4 small topics inside the world. Every week: 3 episodes (A, B, C) on the same small topic.
So the order is never random: every episode builds on the ones before it, and a child who starts with us grows with the channel.

==================================================
1b. ALIGNED WITH AMERICAN PRESCHOOL (PRE-K) LEARNING (added 27 Nov 2026, required by Mahmoud)
==================================================
Our first audience is the USA: parents, preschools, Pre-K and church schools. Everything follows what American early-years teachers expect.
- AMERICAN ENGLISH only, in words and spelling: mom (not mum), pants (not trousers), sweater, pajamas, color, gray, ladybug, sidewalk, trash can, flashlight, store, cookie, truck, fall (autumn is fine too).
- THE FIVE AREAS of the Head Start Early Learning Outcomes Framework (the national framework for ages birth to five) must all be visible across each week:
  1. Approaches to learning: curiosity, trying again, finishing a small task.
  2. Social and emotional development: one value in every episode (sharing, waiting, saying sorry, naming a feeling).
  3. Language and literacy: the target words, a short sentence with each one, a rhyme or a clap for the syllables, and the letter of the week.
  4. Cognition (math and science): count, sort, compare, make a pattern, name a shape or a position word; one simple "I wonder" question (Why is the ice melting?).
  5. Perceptual, motor and physical development: one movement moment in every episode (clap, jump, stretch, point).
- THE WEEK'S STRANDS: every week (A, B, C together) includes ONE letter (the same letter as the Shorts made that week), ONE math skill, ONE feeling or value, and ONE movement game. Episode C is the natural place for the math skill and the letter.
- THE THEMES are the usual American preschool units: All About Me and My Body, My Family, Feelings, Five Senses, Community Helpers, Farm and Zoo Animals, Pets, Plants and Bugs, Weather and Seasons, Ocean, Transportation, Back to School, Food and Nutrition, Colors, Shapes, Numbers, Holidays kept calm and family-centered.
- KINDERGARTEN READINESS, where the three years lead:
  Year 1: about 700 everyday words; count to 10; the basic colors and shapes; follow a one-step direction; name feelings.
  Year 2: short sentences; count to 20; most letters and their sounds; sort and make simple patterns; position words (in, on, under, next to).
  Year 3: tell a short story in order; count to 100 by tens; all 26 letters and sounds; rhymes and first sounds of words; answer who, what, where and why.
- HOW WE TEACH (the methods American teachers use with young children and with children learning English):
  1. Show, say, wait: show the thing, say the word slowly, then wait 3 to 5 seconds in silence so the child answers. Then say it again.
  2. I do, we do, you do: Ian or a parent does it first, then together, then the child alone ("Your turn!").
  3. Whole body: every new verb or thing gets a gesture the child can copy.
  4. Real questions: "What do you see?", "How many?", "What color is it?", and praise for trying ("Good try!", "You did it!").
  5. Repeat with a small change: the same word in three different moments of the story, never three identical scenes.
  6. Calm pace: one idea at a time, no fast cuts, no loud scary sounds.
