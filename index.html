Nurseries & schools

A ready-made circle time, free for your class

Play Ian on the class screen and turn one short story into a whole lesson.

  1. Watch togetherPick the episode that fits this week's topic: animals, plants, shapes or numbers.
  2. Pause and askStop when Ian spots an animal, a shape or a number and let the children call it out.
  3. Keep it goingFinish with drawing, a shape hunt around the room or counting toys.
