For nurseries & schools

Ready-made circle time, free to use in class

Every episode is free on YouTube and set as Made for Kids. Here is how teachers use Ian in a 20-minute lesson.

  1. Watch togetherPlay one episode on the class screen. Each one tells a single short story.
  2. Pause and askStop when Ian finds an animal, a shape or a number and let the children shout it out.
  3. Do it on paperFinish with coloring or counting. Write to us and we will send free printables for your class.
